The Reality of DSA Prep

Why 87% of engineers abandon their DSA spreadsheets

Spreadsheets were built for finance, not cognitive habits. Here is why static Excel prep consistently breaks down:

✕
Flaw #1

The Spreadsheet Graveyard

450 static rows of intimidation

You download Striver or NeetCode in Excel. But raw sheets lack real calendar dates, reminders, or pacing. Within 4 days, looking at static rows feels like data entry, and the tab gets forgotten.

⚠️ 87% of sheets abandoned by Day 4
!
Flaw #2

The "Accepted" Mirage

Passing test cases ≠ Passing the interview

A green checkmark gives false confidence. Candidates regularly write code with subtle O(N²) bottlenecks or overlooked integer overflow edge cases that real MAANG interviewers immediately drill.

⚠️ 73% of rejections stem from Big-O confusion
⚡
Flaw #3

The Backlog Spiral

One busy week breaks the entire plan

Life happens: college exams, work deadlines, or sickness. Missing 2 days derails an Excel schedule. Guilt builds up, the backlog looks overwhelming, and candidates quit entirely.

⚠️ 3 missed days triggers 92% drop-out
👥
Flaw #4

The Isolated Solo Grind

Grinding alone leads to fast burnout

Preparing in total isolation lacks motivation. Without friendly team streaks, peer nudges, or mock oral debate, candidates lose momentum within 2 to 3 weeks.

⚠️ Solo learners quit 4x faster than study pods
